What we do

One partner for your waste, from the bin to the report

We take waste off your hands and turn it into a managed, measurable part of your operation.

On-site waste management

Our team works on your premises to keep waste sorted, contained and moving.

  • Site waste assessment and set-up
  • Sorting and baling at source
  • Bins, skips and a clean waste area
  • Scheduled and on-request collections

Recycling & rebates

Recyclables are a resource. We get them to the right buyers and share the value with you.

  • Cardboard, paper, plastics and metals
  • Rebates paid per kilogram recycled
  • Weighed and recorded every collection
  • Less waste sent to landfill

Reporting & sustainability

Know exactly what your business generates, recycles and sends to landfill.

  • Monthly waste and rebate statements
  • Recycling rate and landfill diversion
  • CO₂e, water and energy savings
  • 24/7 access in your client portal
How we work

Up and running in four steps

Site assessment

We visit, look at your waste streams and volumes, and spot what can be recycled.

Set-up on site

We put the right bins, skips and sorting area in place and agree a collection schedule.

Sort, collect & recycle

Waste is separated at source. Recyclables are weighed and sent for recycling; the rest is disposed of responsibly.

Report & rebate

Each month you get a statement with weights, recycling rate, environmental impact and your rebate.

Client portal

Your waste data, live and ready for your ESG report

Every Harambee client gets a secure login to see their own figures, any time.
